# Understanding your invoice

## Invoice fields

Each invoice displays the invoice number (for example, INV-6001), the amount, the currency (USD or INR for accounts billed in India), the charge date, the status, and the last four digits of the card charged. CloudFlow never stores or shows full card numbers.

## Invoice statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| paid | The charge succeeded |
| failed | The charge was declined; see [KB-BIL-004](#) |
| refunded | A refund approved by the billing team was issued |

## Finding invoices

Owners and admins can view and download a PDF of every invoice in **Settings > Billing > Invoices**.
