# Getting started with CloudFlow

## Overview

CloudFlow automates tasks between the apps your team already uses. A **workflow** consists of a trigger followed by one or more steps. Each time the trigger fires, CloudFlow starts a **run** and records it in the run history.

## Steps

1. Sign in and create a workspace by navigating to **Settings > Workspaces > New workspace**.
2. Open **Workflows > New workflow**. The Flow editor will open.
3. Choose a trigger: an app event, a webhook, or a schedule (KB-ADV-004).
4. Add steps using the **+ Add step** button, then connect the necessary apps for each step (KB-API-005, KB-API-006).
5. Test the workflow by clicking **Test run** with sample data, then **Publish**.

## Version differences

In CloudFlow 3.x, workflows are created from the **Automations > Create** menu and published with **Activate**. The concepts are the same, but the menus differ throughout. Check your version in **Settings > About**.
