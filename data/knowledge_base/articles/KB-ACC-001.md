# Resetting your password

## How it works

Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page, or ask support to start a reset. CloudFlow emails a single-use reset link to the owner email address on file. The link expires after 30 minutes.

## What support can and cannot do

Support can trigger the reset email for the account you are signed in with. We will never show, type or paste a reset link or token in chat, and we cannot send it to a different email address (KB-POL-005).

## If the email does not arrive

Check your spam folder and wait 5 minutes. Each new request invalidates the previous link. After 5 failed sign-in attempts, sign-in is locked for 15 minutes (KB-ACC-002).
