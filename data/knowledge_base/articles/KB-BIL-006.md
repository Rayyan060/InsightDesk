# Changing or cancelling your plan

## Changing plan

Owners can change their plan by navigating to **Settings > Billing > Plan**. Upgrades take effect immediately, while downgrades will apply at the next billing date (KB-BIL-001).

## Cancelling

To cancel your subscription, go to **Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription**. After cancellation, no further invoices will be generated, and your workflows will stop at the end of your paid period. Your workspace will become read-only. Note that cancelling does not delete your data; if you wish to delete your account, refer to KB-POL-004.
