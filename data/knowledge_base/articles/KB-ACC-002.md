# Two-factor authentication and sign-in lockouts

## Turning on two-factor authentication

Navigate to **Profile > Security > Two-factor authentication** and scan the QR code with an authenticator app. Save the 10 recovery codes in a secure place. In **Business** and **Enterprise** workspaces, owners can require two-factor authentication for all members.

## Sign-in lockouts

If you make 5 failed sign-in attempts, your account will be locked for 15 minutes. The easiest way to resolve this is to wait it out. Unfortunately, support cannot shorten the lockout period.

## Version differences

CloudFlow **3.x** supports sign-in with SMS codes only, accessible from **My account > Security**. Starting with **4.0**, you can use authenticator apps for two-factor authentication.
