# Plans and pricing

## Plans

CloudFlow offers four plans: Free, Pro, Business, and Enterprise. The details of prices and limits are outlined in KB-POL-002. The Free plan is always free, while paid plans are billed monthly in advance on the day you first subscribed.

## Upgrading

Upgrading to a new plan takes effect immediately. You will be charged the full monthly price of the new plan on the day of the upgrade, and that day will become your new billing day. Your plan limits will increase right away.

## Downgrading

Downgrading to a lower plan will take effect at the start of your next billing period. If you need to use fewer seats than allowed by the new plan, you should remove any extra members first (KB-GS-002).
