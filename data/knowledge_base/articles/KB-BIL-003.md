# Duplicate and unrecognised charges

## Duplicate charges

A duplicate charge occurs when you receive two paid invoices for the same amount on the same day, even though only one was expected. This often happens when a payment is retried while the first attempt was still processing. Duplicate charges are always reviewed by the billing team, and the duplicate is refunded once a person confirms it. The assistant can help find the two invoices and pass them to billing, but it cannot reverse a charge itself (KB-POL-006).

## Unrecognised charges

To check for unrecognised charges, go to **Settings > Billing > Invoices** and look for the invoice number on your statement. Upgrades are charged on the day they happen, which is the most common reason for an unexpected charge (KB-BIL-001).

## What happens next

The billing queue will respond within 1 business day (KB-POL-003). Please do not open a chargeback while the review is in progress; a chargeback will freeze the invoice.
