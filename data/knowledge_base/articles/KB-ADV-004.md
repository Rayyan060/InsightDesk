# Schedules

## Overview

Schedules allow you to start a workflow at fixed times, such as every weekday at 09:00 in your time zone. They replace cron triggers, which stopped working on June 30, 2026.

## Steps

1. Open the workflow and choose **Trigger > Schedule**.
2. Pick a frequency or enter a cron expression in the **Advanced** tab.
3. Choose the time zone and **Publish**.

## Migrating from cron triggers

Schedules require CloudFlow 4.1 or later. Workspaces on versions 3.x must upgrade to continue running time-based workflows.
