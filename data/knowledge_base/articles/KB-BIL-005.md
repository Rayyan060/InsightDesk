# Requesting a refund

## Before you ask

Refund eligibility is determined by the company's refund policy (KB-POL-001): you can request a refund for paid invoices within 14 days for Pro and Business plans, or 30 days for Enterprise plans. Starting from November 1, 2026, the refund window for Pro and Business plans will be reduced to 7 days.

## How to ask

To request a refund, contact support and specify which invoice you want to be refunded. The support assistant will check the invoice against the policy and forward the request to the billing team with the necessary details. The billing team member makes the final decision on refunds, and the assistant will not provide any promises.

## After you ask

The billing team will respond within 1 business day. If your refund is approved, it will be returned to your original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. The status of the invoice will change to refunded.
