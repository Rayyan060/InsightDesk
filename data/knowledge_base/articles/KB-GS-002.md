# Inviting teammates and managing seats

## Overview

Each person who signs in to a workspace uses one seat. Your plan determines the number of seats available: Free (2), Pro (10), Business (50), and Enterprise (500) (KB-POL-002).

## Inviting a teammate

To invite a teammate, go to **Settings > Members > Invite**, enter their email address, and choose a role (KB-GS-004). The invite remains valid for 7 days. A pending invite does not use a seat until it is accepted.

## When you run out of seats

If every seat is in use, new invites will be blocked with the message 'Seat limit reached.' To free up seats, remove members you no longer need (Settings > Members > ... > Remove) or change your plan (KB-BIL-006). Removing a member will not delete the workflows they built.
