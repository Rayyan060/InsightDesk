# Refund policy

## Introduction

This policy explains when a CloudFlow charge can be considered for a refund and who decides. It applies to every plan that is charged. Free plans are never charged, so they are never refunded.

## Refund window

A paid invoice can be considered for a refund only within the refund window for the account's plan:

| Plan | Refund window |
|---|---|
| Pro | 14 days |
| Business | 14 days |
| Enterprise | 30 days |

The window is counted in calendar days from the charge date. The charge date is day 0, so the **last day of the window is still eligible**: a Pro charge made on 22 September can be considered until the end of 6 October, and a charge made one day earlier cannot. The plan in force when the request is made determines the window.

## Eligibility

Only invoices with the status **paid** can be considered. A **failed** invoice was never collected, so there is nothing to refund. A **refunded** invoice has already been settled. Duplicate charges are always reviewed, whatever their age (see KB-BIL-003).

## Who approves refunds

Refunds and credits are always approved by a person on the CloudFlow billing team. The support assistant can check whether an invoice is inside the window and pass the request to billing with the evidence, but it never issues, promises, or schedules a refund or credit. Every refund request, eligible or not, goes to the billing queue.


## Upcoming changes

From **1 November 2026**, the refund window for Pro and Business plans shortens to **7 days**. Charges made before that date keep the 14-day window that applied when they were made. The Enterprise window stays at 30 days.
