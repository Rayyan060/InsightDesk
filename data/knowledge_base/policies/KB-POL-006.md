# Billing disputes policy

## Introduction

A billing dispute is any disagreement about a charge, such as a duplicate charge, a charge you do not recognize, a charge after cancellation, or an amount that does not match your plan.

## When billing escalates

Billing disputes, refund requests, and credit requests are always handled by the billing queue, with the relevant invoices attached as evidence. The assistant can look up invoices and explain what they show, but cannot reverse charges, issue refunds, or grant credits. The billing queue aims to respond within 1 business day (KB-POL-003).

## Chargebacks

If you open a chargeback with your bank, the disputed invoice will be frozen until the bank decides. We cannot process a refund in parallel with the chargeback.
